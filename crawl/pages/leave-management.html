Leave management

Leave management software and PTO tracker, next to your timesheets

BetterFlow tracks paid time off, sick leave and any other leave type you set up, in the same tool your team logs time in. People ask for time off, a manager approves it, and the balance updates.

Because leave and timesheets live together, nobody has to compare two systems to see who was working on a given day.

How it works

Leave types and balances

  • Set up your own leave types, each with its default number of days and whether it is paid.
  • Each person sees how many days they have left.
  • Let employees whose contract includes paid annual leave take a set number of days in advance (set per company).
  • An admin can correct a balance by hand when needed.

Requests and approvals

  • A request counts working days only, skipping weekends and the public holidays of the person's country.
  • Half days count as half days.
  • Admins hear about a new request, approve or reject it, and the person who asked gets an email either way.
  • A leave can be cancelled, and documents such as a medical note can be attached to it.

Public holidays by country

Holiday calendars are kept per country, so a team spread across several countries gets the right days off for each person without anyone keeping a spreadsheet.

Reports and heads-ups

  • A leave report you can filter and export.
  • Admins get a short note on the morning someone on the team is on leave.
  • Clients see when people on their projects are away, in the client portal.

Time off that is worked back

Some teams let people take a few hours off and make them up later. BetterFlow records those hours, keeps a balance of what is still owed, and has its own report.

The rest of the product, from logging time to client reports, is on the timesheet software page.

Questions

Can people take half a day off?

Yes. A request has a start and end time, and BetterFlow counts half days as well as whole ones.

Are public holidays handled?

Yes. Each person has a country, and the public holidays for that country are left out when the days of a request are counted.

Who is told about a request?

Admins are notified when a request comes in, and the person who asked is told when it is approved or rejected. Admins also get a heads-up on the morning someone is on leave.

Is leave management a separate product?

No. It is part of BetterFlow, at the same price as the timesheets.
