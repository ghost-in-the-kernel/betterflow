INTEGRATIONS & API

Connect your tools, and your other software

Pull activity from the tools your team already uses, and read BetterFlow data from your own systems.

Connect your tools

Link GitHub and Jira per project. BetterFlow pulls commits, tickets, and tracked time to back up every entry.

Does BetterFlow have an API?

Yes. Read-only endpoints return public holiday dates and who is on approved leave on a given day, so other tools and dashboards stay in sync. Health and status endpoints are there for monitoring.

Desktop agent sync

The tracking agent talks to BetterFlow over its own sync API: device registration, batched activity upload, and heartbeats.

MCP

Built for AI assistants

BetterFlow ships an MCP server, so assistants like Claude can answer questions about your projects and timesheets in plain language.

ASK IN PLAIN WORDS

Ask BetterFlow from your terminal

Connect Claude Code or Cursor with a personal token and ask about your projects and timesheets. Answers come from your own BetterFlow data, and the connection is read-only.

Find the gaps

Ask who has no timesheet entry on a project. Weekends, public holidays and approved leave are not counted.

Get a report

Ask for hours per project and who logged the most, for any period of up to 90 days.

Draft a client reply

Ask for a reply built from your real figures. The assistant writes a draft and lists what to check. You review it and send it yourself.
